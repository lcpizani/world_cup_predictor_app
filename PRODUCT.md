# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Football fans (casual to serious) who participate in a World Cup prediction league with friends and colleagues. They visit during or before match days to submit score predictions, track standings, compare against friends, and review tournament statistics. Primary task on any screen: predict, track, compete.

## Product Purpose

A World Cup prediction game where groups of friends or coworkers compete to see who predicts scores most accurately. Users predict match results, earn points based on accuracy, form or join leagues, and follow how the tournament unfolds. Success looks like a group of 5–20 people checking back daily during the tournament.

## Brand Personality

Social, fun, vibrant. The energy of watching football with friends — competitive but not stressful, data-forward but not clinical. The dark navy + gold palette carries authority without going cold or corporate.

## Anti-references

- Flashy sports betting sites: neon colors, animated promos, aggressive conversion patterns, red/green flashing odds. This app has no gambling connotations.
- Flat productivity tools: spreadsheet feel, Notion-style whitespace, clinical data tables without personality. Stats and standings should feel alive, not filed.

## Design Principles

1. **Numbers are the hero.** Score predictions, goal tallies, standings positions — make numeric data feel large, legible, and satisfying to read. The data IS the interface.
2. **Familiar confidence.** Users are scanning quickly between matches. Use standard affordances and predictable layouts so the tool disappears into the task.
3. **Social over solo.** League comparisons, shared predictions, collective stats — the competitive angle is what makes it fun. Design for the group, not just the individual.
4. **Vibrant restraint.** Gold on dark navy has energy without going neon. Keep the palette tight; punch through motion and weight, not through extra colors.
5. **Sport-paced.** Decisions are quick. Hierarchy must be obvious within 2 seconds; primary numbers must be readable at a glance without hunting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Best effort: readable contrast on all text, keyboard-navigable primary flows, respect prefers-reduced-motion. No formal WCAG target.
